About Braincel · TU Darmstadt

Two engineers. One shared craft.

We are Hermann and Amine, two 5th-semester Computer Science students from Darmstadt. We help local businesses in Darmstadt and the surrounding region (+15 km) become more visible online—with modern websites, curated Google profiles, and practical AI tools.

How we work

No handoffs. Just honest work.

Both of us do everything—from initial research and a personal conversation on site to the finished code. By removing middlemen and agency overhead, ideas don't get watered down from the first chat to the working software.

Every message is read and written by us personally. We only use public business data, respect every "no" immediately, and focus on building genuine long-term trust in our city.

Principle 01
Understand first, then build

We listen and ask about the real bottlenecks in day-to-day business before we propose any technical solution.

Principle 02
Fast and practical

Small, immediately working results and clickable prototypes instead of bloated concepts or months of waiting.

Principle 03
Initially free of charge

We start free because we want to gain hands-on experience, build real references, and form lasting partnerships in Darmstadt.
